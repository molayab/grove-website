Refund Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Short version: Grove is free to download and try before you buy. Purchases are sold and processed by Gumroad, which is the merchant of record for the transaction. Statutory consumer-protection rights (see below) are always honored. Beyond that, email hello@molayab.com and we'll review your request — refunds outside the statutory window are considered case-by-case, not guaranteed.

1. Try before you buy

Grove can be downloaded and used as a trial before you pay for anything. We encourage you to install it, connect a real project, and confirm it fits your workflow before purchasing a license.

2. Purchases are sold through Gumroad

Every Grove license is sold through Gumroad, Inc., which acts as the merchant of record for the transaction — Gumroad, not us, is the seller for billing, tax, and payment-dispute purposes, and Gumroad's own Terms of Service govern how it handles refund and chargeback requests. As the license's creator, we can also issue a refund directly from our Gumroad dashboard at our discretion, any time we choose to.

3. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer protection rights you have under the law of your country or state that cannot be waived by agreement — for example, statutory withdrawal rights for digital content purchases in the European Union and United Kingdom (typically 14 days from purchase), or consumer guarantees under Australian Consumer Law. Where local law grants you a right to a refund, that right applies regardless of anything else in this policy. Where you have an EU/UK statutory right of withdrawal for digital content and you ask us to begin providing that content (e.g. by issuing your license key) before the withdrawal period ends, you acknowledge that this right may be lost once the content has been fully delivered, consistent with applicable law.

4. Refunds outside the statutory window

Outside of the statutory rights above, we don't guarantee a refund — but we do look at every request. If something didn't work out (a bug that broke your workflow, a purchase made by mistake, anything else), email us and tell us what happened. We review these case-by-case and would rather make it right than keep money we didn't earn, but there's no fixed window or automatic entitlement past what the law requires.

We reserve the right to decline requests that show a pattern of abuse — for example, repeatedly purchasing and refunding the same license to extend trial access.

5. How to request a refund

Email hello@molayab.com with:

  • The email address used for the purchase
  • Your Gumroad order/receipt ID (found in your purchase confirmation email)
  • What happened — required for requests outside the statutory window, optional otherwise

Approved refunds are issued by Gumroad back to your original payment method, subject to Gumroad's own processing timelines. Once a refund is issued, the associated license key is deactivated and can no longer be used to activate Grove.

6. Chargebacks

If you're unhappy with a purchase, please email us before opening a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or card issuer — it's almost always faster to resolve directly, and a chargeback will result in immediate deactivation of the associated license.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in how Gumroad processes payments. Changes apply to purchases made after the "Last updated" date above; a purchase you already made is governed by the policy in effect at the time you bought it.

8. Contact

Grove is built and supported by an independent developer, Mateo Olaya Bernal. For anything refund-related, reach out at hello@molayab.com.

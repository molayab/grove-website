Features

Everything a modern developer needs.

No Electron. No subscriptions. Just a fast, focused native app.

AI Agents, natively

Bring your own AI coding agent — Claude Code, opencode, Codex, Gemini CLI, or Aider — and it just works, already pointed at the right folder. No PATH to configure, no separate terminal window to babysit.

Worktree-first

Work on three branches at once, without losing your place. Every worktree gets its own agent, shell, files, and diff — click a worktree and everything follows, automatically.

Git panel built in

Do the everyday git stuff without leaving the window. Branch, fetch, pull, push, and commit — plus a recent-commit log so you always know where you left off.

File tree + Diff

Browse your project your way — tree or icon grid — and see exactly what changed, unified or side-by-side, before you commit to anything.

Shell tab

Need a real terminal? It's right there, rooted in the same folder as your agent — run a test, tail a log, copy a path, no context switch.

No destructive defaults

Grove won't let a stray click wipe your work. Force push, branch delete, and discard aren't in here — and anything that reaches beyond your machine asks first.

Keeps working while you don't

Switch to another project and this one keeps going — its agent and shell run on in the background even when you're not looking. Check in on one thing without pausing another, and come back to exactly the tab you left.

Why native?

Built for macOS.
Not ported to it.

  • ✓ Built to never freeze on you — no spinning beachballs, even with several agents running at once.
  • ✓ Feels like part of macOS, not a webpage in a window — matches your Dark Mode, your font size, your system, right out of the box.
  • ✓ Install with confidence — Apple has verified Grove is safe, so you won't hit a scary "unidentified developer" warning, even outside the App Store.
  • ✓ Finds your tools wherever you installed them — Homebrew, ~/.local/bin, ~/.bun/bin — even when you launch Grove from the Dock, not a terminal.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Short version: Downloading and trying Grove doesn't require an account or an email address. This site uses a cookie-free analytics beacon (Cloudflare) on every visit, and — only if you say yes in the cookie banner — Google Analytics, which sets a cookie. Purchases are handled entirely by Gumroad. You can change your cookie choice anytime from the Cookie Settings link in the footer.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers grove.molayab.com and its Spanish translation. Grove the macOS app is built and this site is run by an independent developer, Mateo Olaya Bernal. For anything privacy-related, reach out at hello@molayab.com.

2. Downloading and trying Grove

You can download and use a full trial of Grove without creating an account, without entering an email address, and without providing any personal information. The download itself is served from GitHub Releases; GitHub's own hosting logs apply to that request the same way they would for downloading any file from GitHub.

3. Website analytics

We use two separate analytics tools on this site, and they're treated differently:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics runs on every visit. It's cookie-free — it doesn't set cookies, use local storage, or fingerprint your device — and it only reports aggregate numbers (visits, page views, referrers). Because it doesn't process personal data via cookies, it runs without asking for consent first.
  • Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js) only loads after you click Accept on this site's cookie banner. It sets cookies (see the table below) to help us understand traffic patterns and which parts of the site people actually use. If you click Decline, or never respond, it never loads and never sets a cookie.

Neither tool is used to identify you individually, and we don't combine analytics data with anything else to build a profile of you.

5. Your cookie choice

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether to enable Google Analytics. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so you're not asked again on every page. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page, or by clearing your browser's site data for grove.molayab.com, which resets the choice and shows the banner again on your next visit.

6. Purchases (handled by Gumroad)

License purchases are processed entirely by Gumroad, Inc., which acts as the merchant of record. Gumroad collects and processes whatever payment and billing information a purchase requires (email, payment method, billing address as applicable) under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your full payment card details — Gumroad handles that directly. We do receive purchase confirmation (email address, order ID, license tier) so we can issue and support license keys and process refund requests under our Refund Policy.

7. The Grove app itself

This policy covers the grove.molayab.com website. Grove the macOS app runs locally on your Mac and works with your own git repositories and AI agent credentials — it doesn't send your code or project contents to us. License activation contacts Gumroad to validate your license key.

8. If you email us

If you email hello@molayab.com for support, a refund, or anything else, we keep that correspondence (and any details you choose to include) for as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the interaction.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over data collected about you — for example, to access, correct, delete, or export it, or to object to or withdraw consent for processing (such as Google Analytics cookies, which you can decline or revoke at any time per section 5). This includes rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation and similar laws elsewhere. To exercise any of these, email hello@molayab.com — we'll respond directly rather than through an automated process, since this is a small, independently-run site.

10. International data transfers

Google Analytics and Gumroad are both operated by companies based in the United States, so data they process (analytics events, purchase records) may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where they operate. Each provider maintains its own safeguards for these transfers under its respective policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add or remove an analytics tool. Changes apply from the "Last updated" date above. If a change meaningfully expands what we collect, we'll update the cookie banner so returning visitors see it again.

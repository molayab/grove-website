Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Short version: Downloading and trying Grove doesn't require an account or an email address. This site uses a cookie-free analytics beacon (Cloudflare) on every visit, and — only if you say yes in the cookie banner — Google Analytics, which sets a cookie. Purchases are handled entirely by Gumroad. You can change your cookie choice anytime from the Cookie Settings link in the footer. The Grove app never sends your code anywhere; it can send crash reports (on by default) and anonymous usage analytics (off by default), and you control both in its Settings.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers grove.molayab.com, its Spanish translation, and the data the Grove macOS app sends (section 7). Grove is built and this site is run by an independent developer, Mateo Olaya Bernal. For anything privacy-related, reach out at hello@molayab.com.

2. Downloading and trying Grove

You can download and use a full trial of Grove without creating an account, without entering an email address, and without providing any personal information. The download itself is served from GitHub Releases; GitHub's own hosting logs apply to that request the same way they would for downloading any file from GitHub.

3. Website analytics

We use two separate analytics tools on this site, and they're treated differently:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics runs on every visit. It's cookie-free — it doesn't set cookies, use local storage, or fingerprint your device — and it only reports aggregate numbers (visits, page views, referrers). Because it doesn't process personal data via cookies, it runs without asking for consent first.
  • Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js) only loads after you click Accept on this site's cookie banner. It sets cookies (see the table below) to help us understand traffic patterns and which parts of the site people actually use. If you click Decline, or never respond, it never loads and never sets a cookie.

Neither tool is used to identify you individually, and we don't combine analytics data with anything else to build a profile of you.

5. Your cookie choice

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether to enable Google Analytics. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so you're not asked again on every page. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page, or by clearing your browser's site data for grove.molayab.com, which resets the choice and shows the banner again on your next visit.

6. Purchases (handled by Gumroad)

License purchases are processed entirely by Gumroad, Inc., which acts as the merchant of record. Gumroad collects and processes whatever payment and billing information a purchase requires (email, payment method, billing address as applicable) under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your full payment card details — Gumroad handles that directly. We do receive purchase confirmation (email address, order ID, license tier) so we can issue and support license keys and process refund requests under our Refund Policy.

7. The Grove app itself

Grove runs locally on your Mac and works with your own git repositories and AI agent credentials. It never sends your code, file contents, file names, or prompts to us. The AI agents you run inside Grove talk to their own providers under those providers' terms, exactly as they would in a terminal. The app itself makes these network requests:

  • License checks go to Gumroad to validate your license key.
  • Update checks ask GitHub for the list of Grove releases.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google), on by default. When Grove crashes, it sends the crash log: the stack trace, the app and macOS versions, the Mac model, and a random installation identifier. We use these only to find and fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), off by default. If you turn it on, Grove sends basic app events (first launch, sessions, the app and macOS versions, the Mac model, approximate country, and a random app-instance identifier) plus a license activation or deactivation event with the license price. We use this to understand how many people use Grove and whether they buy it.

You can turn crash reports and usage analytics on or off at any time in Grove › Settings › General › Privacy. Turning usage analytics on or off applies immediately. A crash-report change applies the next time Grove launches, and turning crash reports off also deletes any reports that haven't been sent yet. Neither is used to identify you, and we don't combine them with anything else to build a profile of you.

Older versions: Grove 0.7.1 and earlier don't have this setting and send both crash reports and usage analytics. Update to the latest version to control them.

8. If you email us

If you email hello@molayab.com for support, a refund, or anything else, we keep that correspondence (and any details you choose to include) for as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the interaction.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over data collected about you — for example, to access, correct, delete, or export it, or to object to or withdraw consent for processing (such as Google Analytics cookies, which you can decline or revoke at any time per section 5, or the app's crash reports and usage analytics, which you can turn off in its Settings per section 7). This includes rights under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation and similar laws elsewhere. To exercise any of these, email hello@molayab.com — we'll respond directly rather than through an automated process, since this is a small, independently-run site.

10. International data transfers

Google Analytics, Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics, both by Google), and Gumroad are operated by companies based in the United States, so data they process (analytics events, crash reports, purchase records) may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where they operate. Each provider maintains its own safeguards for these transfers under its respective policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add or remove an analytics tool on this site or in the app. Changes apply from the "Last updated" date above. If a change meaningfully expands what we collect, we'll update the cookie banner so returning visitors see it again.
